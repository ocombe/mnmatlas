Map embed

The quickest way: open a map in the atlas, select a place if you like, and press Embed in the top bar. The dialog writes the code for you. It looks like this:

<iframe src="https://www.mnmatlas.com/underdocks/?embed=map&level=lower&place=wiki-marshall-harding" title="Underdocks · MnM Atlas" width="100%" height="550" style="border:0" loading="lazy" allow="fullscreen; clipboard-write" allowfullscreen></iframe>
  • ?embed=map shows one map on its own: its levels and markers, with no map picker, search menu or editing. An Open in MnM Atlas button and the map's exits open the full atlas in a new tab.
  • The dialog offers three heights: 400, 550 (the default) and 750 px. Any height works; the map fits itself to the frame.
  • Add level= to open on a level or floor and place= to open on a marker or place name (see address options).
  • ?embed=1 embeds the compact full atlas instead, with its map picker and search menu.

Mini map image

For a page that should stay light, such as every NPC page of a wiki, use a small still picture of the spot instead of a frame: 320 × 200 pixels, about 10 KB, with no script. It shows the map around the marker, its pin and its name, and links to the atlas.

<a href="https://www.mnmatlas.com/?find=npc-marshal-harding"><img src="https://www.mnmatlas.com/mini/npc-marshal-harding.webp" width="320" height="200" alt="Marshal Harding on MnM Atlas" loading="lazy" style="border:0"></a>
  • /mini/<wiki id>.webp: for every marker that is linked to its page on the Monsters and Memories Wiki, by the wiki's own id (the id in its NPC data, such as npc-marshal-harding).
  • /mini/<map id>/<place id>.webp: for every marker on the atlas, such as /mini/keepers-bight/farmers.webp.
  • In the atlas, select a marker, press Embed and tick Small still image of this spot instead to get the code for it.
  • The pictures are redrawn every time the atlas is updated, so a moved marker or a corrected name shows up on its own.

When the NPC is not on the atlas yet

You can put the same code on every NPC page, even for NPCs the atlas does not mark yet. When the Monsters and Memories Wiki lists an NPC in a zone the atlas covers but no marker on the atlas is linked to it, /mini/<wiki id>.webp shows this Wanted poster instead of a broken image:

  • The poster's link (?find=<wiki id>) opens the NPC's map on the Wanted board, at its notice, where a reader can place it on the map and claim the bounty.
  • Once someone places the NPC and the marker is published, the next update of the atlas swaps the poster for the real map picture, at the same address: nothing to change on your page.
  • An NPC the wiki does not list in any zone the atlas covers has no picture.

Links and ?find

Any page can link straight to a place on the atlas, by the wiki's id or by name, without knowing which map it is on:

LinkOpens
/?find=npc-marshal-hardingThe map that marks that wiki id, on that marker, at the right level or floor.
/?find=Marshal%20HardingThe same, by name. Case, accents and the kind of apostrophe do not matter.
/?find=enchanter%20trainerA class trainer also answers to "<class> trainer". When several places match, the search menu opens with the text and lists the matches on every map.
/?find=bank&map=underdocksLooks on one map only.
/?find=npc-marshal-harding&embed=mapWorks inside a map embed too.

A wiki id comes first, then an exact name, then names that contain the text. If nothing matches, the atlas says so in its search menu. ?wiki=<wiki id> works the same way as ?find=. An NPC that is not on the atlas yet but is on a map's Wanted board opens that board at its notice (see the Wanted poster).

Address options

OptionWhat it does
/<map id>/Each map has its own address, such as /night-harbor/. The world map is the site's front page. Older ?map=<map id> links still work.
place=<id>Opens on a marker or place name and highlights it. Use Copy link in a popup to get its address.
level=<level id>Opens a map with levels or floors on that one (see the map list). With place=, the place's own level wins.
x= y= z=Opens a view: the centre in map coordinates and the zoom. Share this view in the map toolbar writes them.
find= wiki=Opens on a place found by wiki id or name (see links).
embed=map · embed=1One map on its own, or the compact full atlas, for frames on other sites.

Inside an embed

  • Visitors can drag, zoom, switch levels or floors, and open marker popups. Inside a map embed the scroll wheel only zooms after the map has been clicked, so the page around it still scrolls.
  • Exits to other maps and the Open in MnM Atlas button open the full atlas in a new tab.
  • With ?embed=1 the search menu, the map picker and personal notes are there too. Visitors can sign in with Discord, which opens in a small window so the page stays put, then suggest edits and report problems; suggestions wait for review before they appear.
  • Markers linked to an NPC on the Monsters and Memories Wiki open with the NPC's wiki card (tags, level, race and class, location, notable loot and a short summary, each linking to the wiki) when the frame may show links to that wiki (see wiki links on your site): inside the Monsters and Memories Wiki, yes; on other sites the popup keeps the atlas's own note only.
  • With ?embed=1 the Wanted board is there too: visitors can drag a bounty (an NPC the wiki lists that the map does not mark yet) onto the map and claim it for review. The Wanted board and the wiki suggestions in the Name field appear only where links to the Monsters and Memories Wiki are allowed (on the atlas itself and inside that wiki; see wiki links on your site).
  • The fullscreen button works when the frame allows it (allow="fullscreen", as in the code above).

Wiki links on your site

Markers can link to their wiki page. When the atlas is shown inside another site, it only shows links to that site's own pages, so it never sends your readers to a different wiki:

  • On the Monsters and Memories Wiki (monstersandmemories.wiki): links to its own pages only.
  • On the community wiki at monstersandmemories.miraheze.org: links to its own pages only.
  • On Petrichor's database (monme.no): links to its own pages only.
  • On any other site: no wiki links.

If you run a wiki or database and want your pages linked from the atlas, or the atlas linked differently on your site, open an issue on the atlas's GitHub page.

For wiki owners

On the Monsters and Memories Wiki, the atlas does more than link: the note form suggests the wiki's NPCs as people type a name, the Wanted board lists the wiki's NPCs that the atlas does not mark yet, marker popups show a short card for the NPC, and the still images show a Wanted poster for NPCs not placed yet. Another wiki or database can get the same, on the atlas and inside its own pages. Here is what the atlas needs from it:

  • A key and a rate limit. The atlas keeps the key on its own servers, never in the page. It asks the wiki when the atlas is published, not for each visitor; the name suggestions of signed-in people go through the atlas's server, each account limited. Today that is about 100 requests a minute at most.
  • Stable ids and page addresses for NPCs (and items). The atlas stores the id and the link with a marker, never the wiki's text.
  • NPC search by name, within a zone, returning for each result its id, name, page address, role (merchant, quest giver, named, trainer), level, a short location line and tags (such as "Named · Human · Wizard", with a trainer's class).
  • Every NPC of a zone in one request, with the same fields, for the Wanted board. The wiki's zone names should match the atlas's maps, or come with a list that matches them.
  • One NPC by id, adding its race and class, a short summary (a couple of sentences) and its loot: each item's name, page address and drop rate when known. This makes the cards in marker popups.
  • Permission to show these fields on markers, cards and the Wanted board, each linking back to its page on the wiki, with the wiki credited. The atlas never copies full descriptions or mirrors the wiki.

Inside the wiki's own pages, embeds then show its links, cards and Wanted board, and never another wiki's. To set this up, open an issue on the atlas's GitHub page.

Credits

MnM Atlas is an illustrated, unofficial fan atlas, not an official game map. Many maps build on community maps whose authors are credited on each map (the credits line on the map and About in the atlas). When you show the atlas on your site, keep the embed's own credits visible, or for a still image keep its link to the atlas, and credit "MnM Atlas" and the original map authors where you describe it.

Data from the Monsters and Memories Wiki used in the atlas (NPC names and links, the Wanted lists and the NPC cards in popups) is credited and linked back to the wiki as it asks; the atlas never copies the wiki's full descriptions.
